The four rules

A
Sentence , FANBOYS sentence.

Two complete sentences (each has its own subject + verb) → comma before the FANBOYS word.

I was tired, but I finished my homework.

B
Sentence FANBOYS verb / word.

The second part has no new subject → no comma.

I was tired no ,but happy.

C
Subordinate clause , main clause.

The subordinate clause comes first → comma after it.

Because it rained, we stayed home.

D
Main clause subordinate clause.

The subordinate clause comes last → usually no comma.

We stayed home no ,because it rained.

Coordinating conjunctions

FANBOYS

For
reason (= because) · 因為

Please be quiet, for the baby is sleeping.

Note: "for" as a conjunction always joins two sentences.

And
adds information · 和、而且

Dad cooked dinner, and Mom washed the dishes.

Dad cooked dinner no ,and washed the dishes.

Nor
adds a second negative · 也不

Leo did not call me, nor did he send a message.

After "nor," the verb comes before the subject (did he).

But
contrast · 但是

The night market was crowded, but the food was delicious.

My cat is small no ,but very brave.

Or
choice · 或者

We can take the MRT, or we can ride our bikes.

We can take the MRT no ,or ride our bikes.

Yet
surprising contrast · 然而

She studied hard, yet she failed the test.

He is quiet no ,yet friendly.

So
result · 所以

It started to rain, so we went inside.

Don't mix up "so" (result) with "so that" (purpose, Rule D).

Subordinating conjunctions

Subordinators by use

Flip test: When I got home, my dog ran to the door.

My dog ran to the door no ,when I got home.
